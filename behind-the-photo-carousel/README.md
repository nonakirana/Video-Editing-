# Carousel: "Behind the team photo"

Draft Instagram carousel for Nona Kirana / Barakah Homes. One team photo per slide, with
"what you see" and "what you don't" copy. Status: storyboard for review.

- `storyboard.png` — contact sheet of all 10 draft slides.
- `storyboard.md` — slide copy, and the details to confirm before the final build.
- `slides/01.png` … `slides/10.png` — draft renders, 1080x1350.
- `make_carousel.py` — regenerates the slides (`python3 make_carousel.py`). Photos are read from `../recruitment-carousel/photos/`.
- `make_storyboard.py` — rebuilds `storyboard.png` from the slides.
