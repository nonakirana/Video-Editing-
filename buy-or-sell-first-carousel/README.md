# Carousel: "Buy first, or sell first?"

Nona Kirana / Barakah Homes @ PropNex version of an 8-slide explainer on whether to buy or
sell first when upgrading. Rebuilt in the navy, gold and cream scheme with Nona's photos.

- `slides/01.png` … `slides/08.png` — ready to upload, 1080×1350.
- `storyboard.png` — contact sheet of all 8 slides.
- `caption.md` — caption, hashtags and posting notes.
- `make_carousel.py` — regenerates the slides (`python3 make_carousel.py`).
- `make_storyboard.py` — rebuilds `storyboard.png`.
- `photos/nona-*.jpg` — portrait crops used in the polaroid frames. Swap these for your own
  portraits (3:4 works best) and re-run the script. Logos are read from `../recruitment-carousel/photos/`.
- `buy-or-sell-first-carousel.zip` — slides, storyboard and caption bundled for download.
