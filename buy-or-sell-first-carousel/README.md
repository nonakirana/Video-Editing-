# Carousel: "Sell first or buy first? Wrong question."

Nona Kirana / Barakah Homes @ PropNex carousel on the order of selling and buying when
upgrading. Inspired by a PropNex explainer on the same topic, but with its own structure
and wording: the "gap" framing, when each order works, the overlap timeline, the numbers
to check, and common mistakes. Navy, gold and cream scheme with Nona's consult photos.

- `slides/01.png` … `slides/08.png` — ready to upload, 1080×1350.
- `storyboard.png` — contact sheet of all 8 slides.
- `caption.md` — caption, hashtags and posting notes.
- `make_carousel.py` — regenerates the slides (`python3 make_carousel.py`).
- `make_storyboard.py` — rebuilds `storyboard.png`.
- `photos/` — source photos and the 3:4 portrait crops used in the polaroid frames
  (`nona-desk-consult.jpg`, `nona-walkthrough.jpg`, `nona-office-consult.jpg`, `nona-ipad-consult.jpg`;
  `nona-award.jpg`, `nona-desk-consult-2.jpg`, `nona-condo-viewing.jpg` and `nona-cafe-consult.jpg` are spares). Logos come from `../recruitment-carousel/photos/`.
- `buy-or-sell-first-carousel.zip` — slides, storyboard and caption bundled for download.
