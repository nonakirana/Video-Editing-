Applies to every reel

Look, captions and sound

Captions

Burned-in, 2–4 words per line, bold sans, lower-middle of frame (clear of the TikTok/Reels UI). Key words in gold. Your Malay kept as spoken, light clean-up of fillers only.

naik $500,000

Cuts and movement

Every jump cut is hidden with an alternating 100% / 112% punch-in. One speed-ramp per reel at most. Freeze-frames only on the two comedic/emotional beats marked above.

Colour

The green office wall is neutralised (white balance), skin warmed slightly, highlights on the white hijab protected, light vignette. Clean, bright, not filtered.

Graphics use the Barakah Homes navy and gold from your carousel so the feed stays consistent.

Sound

Lav audio de-noised and levelled to −14 LUFS. Music bed sits well under the voice: upbeat and light for Reels 1, 4, 5; slower and more cinematic for Reel 2; soft for Reel 3. Whoosh / pop / ka-ching SFX only where a graphic lands.

Graphics, not stock footage

All added visuals are motion graphics built in-house: counters, price tags, map pins, HDB-vs-condo icons, CPF card, checklist, comparison cards. No stock footage is needed. If you want real B-roll (condo pool, HDB blocks, Jurong West), send me a few clips or photos and I will weave them in.

Your smiling frame behind a navy panel: Barakah Homes + PropNex logos, @nonakirana, the phone number you say on camera. Please confirm the number: I heard “8268 111”, which is one digit short.
